## Article 4. Service Description and Plans

### 4.1 Free Plan

The Free plan lets you use the following features.

- Voice recording (M4A format)
- On-device speech-to-text transcription
- Writing notes (markdown editing; in the note editor, photos, files, document scans, and tags — the summary editor accepts text only)
- Summary editing (editing a summary that has already been created)
- Export (text sharing, Markdown, PDF)
- Search and folders, Siri and Shortcuts

Cloud AI features are not available under the Free plan, and no audio or text data is transmitted to the Operator's servers, to OpenAI, or to Apple (Private Cloud Compute).

### 4.2 Pro Plan (Subscription)

The Pro plan includes all Free plan features plus:

- Speaker diarization (on-device)
- On-device AI summarization (powered by Apple Foundation Models)
- OpenAI GPT-powered AI summarization (text only transmitted; no audio transmitted)
- Private Cloud Compute AI summarization (when you choose it under the "AI Engine" setting in Settings; text only transmitted; no audio transmitted — see Article 6-3 of the Privacy Policy)
- OpenAI-powered transcription text correction (text only transmitted)
- OpenAI-powered multilingual meeting translation (text segments only transmitted)
- Polish Note (polishes the text of a note with the engine you choose under "AI Engine" in Settings — on-device, Private Cloud Compute, or OpenAI GPT; cloud engines receive only the title and text of the note (including the titles, file names, and link text of the cards in it), and photo files are not transmitted — see Articles 6-2 and 6-3 of the Privacy Policy)
- Note links and inserting recording links into notes
- Calendar event linking
- Audio file import
- iCloud synchronization (real-time data and storage optimization)
- MCP Local Integration (macOS only, default OFF) — lets another program you connect directly on this Mac query your recording and note library, edit a recording's title, folder, and domain, and create or edit notes (audio files not provided — see Article 6-4 of the Privacy Policy)
- System Audio Capture (macOS only) — captures the audio of another app you directly select on the "New Recording" screen, and additionally records the microphone if you turn on "Include My Voice" (see Article 6-5 of the Privacy Policy)

Under the Pro plan, transcribed text and the title and text of notes (not audio files) may be transmitted to OpenAI's API or to Private Cloud Compute for processing. Which of the two applies follows the "AI Engine" setting in Settings (if the setting has not been changed, the default engine is GPT), and if you choose "On-Device", summaries are processed only on this device and nothing is transmitted. **Audio files are never transmitted to the Operator's servers, to OpenAI, or to Apple** (if a Pro subscriber enables iCloud sync, audio files are stored on the user's own Apple iCloud).

**Usage Limits**

OpenAI-powered AI summarization, text correction, multilingual translation, and Polish Note within the Pro plan are subject to a monthly usage limit. When you reach the usage limit, these features become unavailable until your usage resets at the start of the next billing cycle. Private Cloud Compute AI summarization and Polish Note (when the "AI Engine" setting is Private Cloud Compute) are subject to a separate limit that Apple sets on its own; the exact reset cycle is not publicly disclosed. On-device features (speech-to-text transcription, speaker diarization, on-device AI summarization, and on-device Polish Note) are always available regardless of usage.

**Pricing**

- Monthly: KRW 6,600
- Annual: KRW 55,000

Prices may vary by region and currency. The applicable price will be displayed in the App Store at the time of purchase.

## Article 10. Data Processing and Privacy

1. **Processing Without Cloud AI (Free Plan).** Cloud AI features are not available under the Free plan, and no audio or text data is transmitted to the Operator's servers, to OpenAI, or to Apple (Private Cloud Compute). Transcription is processed on-device.

2. **Cloud Processing (Pro Plan).** Under the Pro plan, transcribed text (and, for Polish Note, the title and text of a note) may be transmitted to OpenAI's API for enhanced summarization, text correction, multilingual translation, and Polish Note (the default engine for AI summarization is GPT). In addition, if you choose Private Cloud Compute under the "AI Engine" setting, AI summarization and Polish Note may be processed via Apple Inc.'s Private Cloud Compute — this path is transmitted directly from your device to Apple's servers without passing through the Operator's backend, and is subject to a separate usage limit set by Apple. If you choose "On-Device" under "AI Engine", processing takes place only on this device and nothing is transmitted (the engines for voice summaries and for Polish Note are chosen separately, and in Polish Note, "On-Device" does not rewrite the note: the device model only picks the to-do lines and adds a checkbox in front of them, leaving the text unchanged). If the On-Device engine cannot be used or cannot process the note, or if Private Cloud Compute declines the content, the app does not switch to the cloud on its own: it first asks you and transmits only that one time, after you agree. For voice summaries, "Automatic" can no longer be newly chosen and remains only for users who had already chosen it before the app update; with that setting the app uses the first available engine in the order on-device → Private Cloud Compute → GPT (in this case the app does not ask before moving on to another engine, and moving on to GPT draws on your usage allowance — see Article 6-3 of the Privacy Policy). Audio files are never transmitted to the Operator's servers, to OpenAI, or to Apple, and photo files are not transmitted in Polish Note either. This transmission occurs only after the separate consent for the processing of sensitive information under **Article 6-6** of the Privacy Policy has been given; if you decline or withdraw that consent, the cloud AI features above stop working. See Articles 6-2, 6-3, and 6-6 of the Privacy Policy for details.

3. **Calendar Event Linking and Automatic Matching (Pro, opt-in).** Recordings may be automatically matched with events in the Calendar app (EventKit). This feature requires Calendar permission. Event data is processed on-device only and is never transmitted to the Operator's servers (for Pro subscribers, the matching metadata syncs only to the user's own Apple iCloud). Core functionality (recording, transcription, summarization) continues to work without this permission.

4. **Analytics and Crash Reporting.** The App uses Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics to collect anonymized usage data and crash reports for the purpose of improving the Service. This data does not include recordings or transcription content. The App does not currently provide an in-app toggle to disable Firebase Analytics; to opt out, please contact kdwara@icloud.com.

5. **Backend Infrastructure.** The Operator operates Google Firebase Cloud Functions (Seoul region — asia-northeast3, project `cleio-prod`) for backend functions including OpenAI API proxy, subscription verification, and usage management. This infrastructure is invoked only when Pro cloud features are requested. Private Cloud Compute AI summarization does not pass through this infrastructure; it is transmitted directly from your device to Apple's servers.

6. **iCloud Sync (Pro Plan).** Pro subscribers may use iCloud synchronization and storage optimization. Data is stored on Apple's iCloud infrastructure, governed by Apple's privacy policies.

7. **MCP Local Integration (Pro, User's Choice, macOS only).** You may allow another program you connect directly on this Mac to query your recording and note library (transcripts, summaries including any to-dos, notes, folder list, etc.; audio and photo files excluded) and edit a recording's title, folder, and domain, and create notes, add to them, or replace their whole body (replacing a note's body can remove its existing content and photos from the note). The default is OFF, and access is protected by a server that responds only to this Mac and an access token known only to you. How a program you connect subsequently handles that data is outside the Operator's control and is governed by that program's own terms and privacy policy. See Article 6-4 of the Privacy Policy for details.

8. **System Audio Capture (Pro, User's Choice, macOS Only).** You may capture the audio output of another app you directly select on the "New Recording" screen, and the microphone is additionally recorded if you turn on "Include My Voice." Captured audio is processed the same way as microphone recordings — it is stored and processed on this Mac and is not transmitted to the Operator's servers, to OpenAI, or to Apple; it is stored and synced to your own Apple iCloud Drive only if you, as a Pro subscriber, have enabled iCloud sync. A persistent visual indicator is shown in the menu bar and the recording screen while capture is in progress. The app you capture may include the voices of third parties, such as call or meeting participants, and complying with applicable law in that regard is your responsibility under Article 7. See Article 6-5 of the Privacy Policy for details.

9. **Privacy Policy.** For detailed information about our data practices, please refer to the Privacy Policy available within the App.
